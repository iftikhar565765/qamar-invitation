The Chronicle

Two hearts, one dua answered

Every blessed story begins with a dua whispered quietly, and ours was whispered on a December night on the university rooftop, over one shared cup of Kashmiri chai and a sky full of stars neither of us was studying. One conversation that outlasted the last tram home, one umbrella raced through the winter rain — and somehow, that night decided the rest of our lives.

Five years and two cities later, our families gathered beneath one roof to give their blessing, and the date was written with dates and water — the sunnah way. Nothing would mean more than to have you standing beside us as we say Qubool Hai, and begin a home built on sakinah, mawaddah and mercy.
